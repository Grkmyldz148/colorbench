Every invertible color space in colour-science — plus helmlab — ranked on 46 real psychophysical datasets. Lower score = closer to human vision.

There is no single overall winner by design — no color space is best at everything. The rank column is the mean of the per-property ranks; the real story is each column. Ak koyun kara koyun.

This is the perceptual-measurement board (how well a space's geometry predicts human difference / hue / discrimination). The CAM-UCS family leads it — they are appearance models fit to exactly this kind of data. helmlab is a generation space, tested here off its home turf, and still wins hue outright; its own arena (gamut/gradient quality) is a separate axis where it beats OKLab 26–8.
