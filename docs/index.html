Color spaces ranked on real psychophysical datasets — from colour-science and helmlab. Lower score = closer to human vision.

Two boards, on purpose. A space that measures color difference and a space that generates color are judged on different things. Mixing them into one ranking is a category error — so we don't. helmlab isn't one entry either: it competes as metricspace (a learned difference model) on the measurement board and genspace (a synthesis space) on the generation board. Measurement = STRESS on the real difference datasets (COMBVD, MacAdam 1974), each model scoring with its own ΔE — this is a pure "predict human color difference" contest. Generation = synthesis geometry: sRGB round-trip precision, gamut-mapping hue monotonicity and ΔE smoothness, plus two human-data geometry checks (constant-hue straightness, Munsell spacing). Colour-science spaces use their canonical reference implementations.
